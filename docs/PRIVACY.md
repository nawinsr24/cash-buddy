# Cash++ privacy policy (draft for review)

**What Cash++ stores.** Your accounts, transactions, debts, investments and budget.
They are kept on your phone in an encrypted database.

**Bank alerts (Android).** If you allow it, Cash++ uses Android's notification access to see alerts from your messaging app and turns bank transaction alerts into entries for you to confirm. It never opens your message inbox. Notifications from other apps are ignored immediately and never stored. Matching alerts wait on your phone only until the app checks them. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**Google Drive backup (optional).** If you turn it on, your data is encrypted on the phone
with a backup password only you know and then saved in a hidden app folder in your own
Google Drive. We cannot read it, and we cannot recover it if you forget the backup password.

**What we collect.** Nothing. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts with us.
The app downloads one small settings file from our website to switch features on or
off; no information about you is sent with that request.

**Permissions.** Notifications (daily reminder), notification access (Android only, optional, for bank alerts),
biometrics / screen lock (optional app lock), internet (Drive sync and the settings file).

**Your control.** You can delete the data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling,
and delete the Drive copy from your Google account's app data settings.

**Not financial advice.** Scores, titles and summaries are habit guides, not advice.
