What it does

  • Four-pillar dashboard: Spending, Income, Incoming Debts, Liabilities, for any date range
  • Combined or split (cash / bank) accounts, cash-vs-card tracking, two-level categories
  • Loans you gave or took, with simple or compound interest and partial repayments
  • Review bank messages you paste in; nothing is added until you confirm
  • Light and dark mode follow your phone
